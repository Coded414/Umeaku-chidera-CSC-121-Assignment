UNIVERSITY GUIDELINES

Rules & Regulations

Students are expected to follow the rules of the university and maintain a respectful academic environment.

01

Academic Discipline

Students must attend lectures regularly and participate actively in academic activities.

02

Respect for Others

Every student must treat staff and fellow students with respect and dignity.

03

Proper Identification

Students should carry their valid university identification cards when on campus.

04

Dress Code

Students are expected to dress appropriately and maintain decent appearance on campus.

05

Protection of University Property

University facilities, equipment and property must be handled responsibly.

06

Academic Integrity

Cheating, plagiarism and other forms of academic dishonesty are prohibited.
